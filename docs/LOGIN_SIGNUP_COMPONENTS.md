# 2013 Login and Signup Pages

The Login page structure follows the 2013-07-06 `Login/Default.aspx` capture.
The Signup structure/copy follows the 2013-12-05 `Login/Signup.aspx` capture.
The pages reuse the sourced 2013 navigation, footer, and launcher component.

Facebook login, advertisements, tracking, guest-account implementation, and
external certification embeds are excluded because they are unavailable or not
safe local dependencies. Birthday/gender fields preserve the capture structure;
their persistence is intentionally deferred until the user-profile schema is
expanded.
